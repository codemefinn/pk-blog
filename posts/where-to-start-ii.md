Once you've set up the background and Page element, it's time to start adding elements to bring your site to life.

Among Carrd's elements, there's one that's especially useful: the **Container**. But when should you use it, and what can it do for your layout?

## 1. Apply a Different Style or Background

Use a Container when you want a section of your site to have a different look from the page background. You can give it its own background color, image, or other styling.

## 2. Keep Elements Organized

Placing elements inside a Container makes it easier to manage their positioning and alignment. It also helps keep related content together as your site grows.

## 3. Create Columns and Structured Layouts

By default, elements on a Page are arranged vertically, with each element taking up its own row.

A Container gives you more flexibility. You can arrange elements into columns and give each group its own space, making it easier to build more structured layouts.

Once you get comfortable with Containers, you'll find them useful for much more than simply grouping elements.
