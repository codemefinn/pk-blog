When starting a new Carrd site, I usually follow a simple order: decide on the background first, then set up the Page element. These two choices help establish the overall look and structure of your site.

## 1. Decide on the Background Style

Start by choosing the overall background for your site. It could be a solid color, an image, a slideshow, or something else.

Since the background covers the entire screen, it's a good idea to settle on this before working on the content.

## 2. Set Up the Page Element

Think of the Page element as the canvas where all your site content lives.

Carrd offers different page styles, including **Default** and **Box** (Wide Box / Tall Box). So, what's the difference, and which one should you choose?

### Box

The Box style lets you customize the page itself. You can set a background color, adjust margins, add borders, and more.

Think of it as creating a card-like layout that holds your content.

### Default

The Default style focuses more on the content itself. It doesn't offer the same direct styling options for the page, making it a good choice when your content naturally forms the overall layout.

Both styles can achieve similar results, but this is how I usually think about them when starting a new design. Choose the one that best fits the layout you're going for.
