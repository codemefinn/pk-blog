Let's take a closer look at the Carrd **Slideshow** element and how its aspect ratio settings affect the way images are displayed.

## Preserve Aspect Ratio

When **Preserve Aspect Ratio** is enabled, the slideshow behaves similarly to the Gallery's **Full** style: images are displayed without being cropped.

You can still adjust the display proportion of each image individually, giving you more control over how they appear.

## Appearance → Aspect Ratio: Strict

When the slideshow's **Aspect Ratio** is set to **Strict**, the slideshow maintains a fixed aspect ratio.

As its width changes across different screen sizes, its height adjusts accordingly to preserve that ratio.

This keeps the visible area consistent, so visitors see the same framing of each image regardless of the screen size.

## When Should You Use It?

This setting is particularly useful when the first **Preserve Aspect Ratio** option is disabled and you want to avoid automatic cropping.

Without a fixed aspect ratio, different screen sizes may reveal different parts of an image. Setting the slideshow to **Strict** helps maintain a consistent visual presentation across devices.
