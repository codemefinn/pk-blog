### Your Carrd Website Can Be More Than a Landing Page
You've built your website with Carrd. You have the design you love, the layout just right, and everything looks the way you want.
But what if you also want to publish articles, share updates, or build a collection of content over time?

Carrd makes it easy to create beautiful websites, but it wasn't designed to store and manage a growing collection of content. Setting up an entirely separate blogging platform just to publish articles, however, may be more than you need.

What if you could keep your website on Carrd and manage your articles somewhere else, without adding unnecessary complexity?

### A Different Way to Blog with Carrd and GitHub
That's where GitHub comes in.

GitHub is a platform where you can store and manage files. Instead of using a traditional blog dashboard, you keep your articles as simple Markdown files — a format some Carrd users may already be familiar with — in your own GitHub repository.

Think of it as a folder for all your blog posts. You can create a new article, edit an existing one, or organize your content whenever you need to — without maintaining another website or content management system.

With **PK Blog**, three components work together to make blogging on Carrd possible:

- **GitHub provides your content workspace.** Start with our ready-to-use repository template and manage your articles as Markdown files.
⭣
- **PK Blog handles content delivery.** It reads your articles from GitHub, processes them, and makes them available for your Carrd website.
⭣
- **Carrd brings your blog to life.** Use your existing Carrd website to control the design, layout, typography, and reading experience.
