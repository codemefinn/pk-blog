When I first started using Carrd, one thing that used to bother me was how long it took to scroll through the font and icon lists to find what I wanted.

Then I discovered a little shortcut: once the list appears, simply type the **first letter** of the font or icon name. Carrd will jump to matching entries, making it much quicker to find what you're looking for.

It's a small trick, but one that can save you quite a bit of time when working on your site!
